ONBOARDING GUIDE FOR NEW EMPLOYEES
People & Culture team, edition 2026
Rheintal Antriebstechnik GmbH


# 1. Welcome

Welcome to Rheintal Antriebstechnik GmbH. We are a family-owned company founded in 1961 in Lörrach with around 850 employees at three sites: Lörrach (headquarters and hydraulics plant), Weil am Rhein (power electronics) and Brno in the Czech Republic (assembly). This guide walks you through your first 90 days.


# 2. Your first day

Please arrive at the main reception, Industriestraße 14, at 9:00. Bring your ID card, your tax identification number, your social security card and your bank details. You will receive your employee badge, your laptop and your locker key. The badge opens the doors from 6:00 to 20:00; outside these hours a request to Facility Management is required.


# 3. Working hours and time recording

Full-time employees work 35 hours per week under the collective agreement. Core working hours are 9:00 to 15:00 on Monday to Thursday and 9:00 to 13:00 on Friday. Flexitime is possible between 6:00 and 20:00. Working time is recorded in the HR portal; the flexitime account may not exceed plus 40 or minus 20 hours. Working students may work a maximum of 20 hours per week during the lecture period and up to 40 hours during semester breaks.


# 4. Remote work

After the probation period of 6 months, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager. Working students can work remotely from day one on days without on-site tasks. Remote work from abroad requires HR approval and is limited to 20 days per year within the EU.


# 5. Vacation and absence

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Vacation days | 30 days per year for full-time, pro rata for part-time |
| Carry-over | Remaining days must be taken by 31 March of the following year |
| Sick leave | Inform your manager before 9:00; certificate needed from day 3 |
| Special leave | 1 day for moving, 2 days for own wedding, 2 days for a bereavement |


# 6. IT and accounts

Your Microsoft 365 account is ready on your first day. Set up multi-factor authentication with the Microsoft Authenticator app before you leave on day one. The IT service desk is reachable at extension 4444 from 7:00 to 17:00. Please read the IT Security Guideline in your first week and complete the security e-learning within 30 days.


# 7. Training in the first 90 days

- Week 1: company tour, safety briefing, introduction to the ERP system.
- Week 2 to 4: shadowing in your team, first small tasks, meet your buddy weekly.
- Day 30: first feedback talk with your manager.
- Day 60: completion of mandatory e-learnings (security, compliance, data protection).
- Day 90: probation review and goal setting for the rest of the year.


# 8. Benefits

Employees receive a subsidised canteen lunch for 4.20 Euro, a JobRad bicycle leasing offer, a company pension contribution of 50 Euro per month and the Deutschlandticket job ticket with a 30 Euro subsidy. Working students receive the canteen subsidy and the job ticket subsidy but no pension contribution.
